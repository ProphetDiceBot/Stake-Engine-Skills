4. Visual Decision StandardsSilhouette Integrity: When referencing competitor styles, use style-only prompts. Never feed competitor symbols as direct structural img2img anchors. Keep the original project silhouette for direct A/B validation.Keying Discipline:Use green screen for warm, gold, red, or magenta subjects.Use magenta screen for green/cyan vegetation and gems.Never run erosion/choke algorithms on translucent lighting, glows, or particle fields—use scripts/key_light.py to preserve additive straight alpha.Canvas Registration: Maintain identical canvas dimensions, anchor points, and offsets across an entire symbol set or animation sequence. Never crop or auto-trim individual frames independently.Lighting Coherence: Render secondary lighting, winning state flares, or tier recolors from a single master model. Independent random generations do not produce temporally stable frame sequences.Contrast Testing: Review all transparent cutouts at 100% and 50% scale against both #000000 (black) and #FFFFFF (white) backplates to detect dark fringing, matte halo spill, or clipped outer glows.Advisory AI Scores: AI review scores are guidance, not automated blockers. Never resubmit unchanged artwork repeatedly to "game" a score. An incomplete critique response counts as a failure, not an approval.5.
